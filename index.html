Detailed Unit Topics & Syllabus Outline

Click unit to expand / collapse

Topics: Electric Field lines, electric flux, Gauss's law (Integral & differential forms), applications to linear, plane, and spherical charge distributions. Conservative & irrotational nature of E. Electric potential, relation between V and E, potential energy of a system of charges, energy density, potential for spherical charge distribution.

Gauss's Law Formulations: Differential (∇ · E = ρ/ε₀) and Integral form.
Field Conservatism: Irrotational field condition (∇ × E = 0) & E = -∇V.

Topics: Magnetic field B, magnetic flux, Biot-Savart's law (straight conductor), Force on a point charge, properties of B (curl & divergence), solenoidal field, Integral form of Ampere's law (straight, circular, solenoidal currents). Energy stored in magnetic field, force between parallel conductors, Magnetic field intensity. Ballistic Galvanometer (torque, working, current/charge sensitivity, electromagnetic damping, critical damping resistance).

Biot-Savart & Ampere: Field evaluation for straight wire, loop & solenoids.
Ballistic Galvanometer: Complete theory, damping & charge sensitivity.

Topics: Faraday's laws (differential & integral), Lenz's law, self and mutual induction. Continuity equation, modification of Ampere's law, displacement current, Maxwell's equations in vacuum & dielectric medium, boundary conditions, plane wave equation, transverse nature of EM waves, velocity of light, Poynting's theorem.

Maxwell's Field Equations: Displacement current and boundary conditions.
Wave Propagation: Transverse EM wave equation & Poynting theorem (S = E × H).

Topics: Growth and decay of currents in LR, CR, and LCR circuits, critical damping. AC relation between I and V in pure R, C, and L, vector diagrams, power in AC circuits, LCR series and parallel resonant circuit, Q-factor, AC & DC motors (single & three-phase basics). Passive/active elements, power sources, T and Pi transformations, Superposition, Thevenin's, Norton's, Reciprocity, and Maximum Power Transfer theorems.

AC Resonance: LCR series & parallel resonance and Q-factor derivations.
Theorems: Thevenin, Norton, Superposition, Maximum Power Transfer & Reciprocity.
